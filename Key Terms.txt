Figure is the blank canvas that holds the whole picture.

Axes is the actual chart area inside the canvas where the data is drawn.

Pyplot is the Python module used to build the plots.

Subplot is a single chart inside a grid of multiple charts.

Title is the main heading at the top of the graph.

Label is the text on the X or Y axis explaining what is being measured.

Legend is the key box showing what each color or line stands for.

Color is the hue used for your lines, bars, or points.

Line Plot connects points with a line to show trends over time.

Bar Chart uses bars to compare different categories.

Scatter Plot places individual dots to show relationships between two variables.

Histogram groups numbers into buckets to show frequency.

Ticks are the small numbers or marks along the axes.

Grid shows faint lines in the background to make reading values easier.

DPI controls the image quality when saving your plot.

Tight Layout automatically stops titles and labels from overlapping.
